Idyll

Cygnus olor, the mute swan, young

A small, quiet app that takes the ads, Shorts, Reels and algorithmic feeds out of YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X, and leaves your friends exactly where they were.

CallNone. Named for the mute swan.
HabitatiPhone, Android, Chrome and Mac. Arriving soon.
DietMessages, search, and whatever friends send you.
AvoidsThe feed, the autoplay, the sponsored post.
Cost to observeNothing, for good.

Range

Where it lives, and what it leaves behind

YouTube

Video ads, Shorts, the home feed. Subscriptions and search remain.

Instagram

Sponsored posts, Reels, Explore. Messages and the reel a friend sent remain.

Facebook

Reels, Watch, the algorithm. Friends, in order, remain.

X

For you, promoted posts, trends. Following remains.

Behaviour

Shy by nature. It can’t read your pages.

On iPhone, Idyll hands Safari a list of rules and keeps its distance; iOS shows it can’t read or send anything from the pages you visit. You log in to the real sites and the real apps, never inside Idyll. No account. Nothing leaves your phone.

Observed carrying: 0 trackers.

Field notes

How to spot it

On iPhone, as a Safari blocker that cleans the websites, and a daily allowance for the real apps: messages and notifications keep arriving until it runs out.

On Android, as a clean browser, and a feed guard inside the real apps that skips ads and stops a swipe to the next Short or Reel.

On Chrome and Mac, as the same rules, on your laptop.

Limits

What it won’t do

No app can change what’s inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone; Apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. Idyll cleans up the websites, and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
