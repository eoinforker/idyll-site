It can’t read your pages.

On iPhone, Safari applies Idyll’s rules itself, and iOS shows it can’t read or send anything from the pages you visit. You log in to the real sites and the real apps, never inside Idyll.

account: none · trackers: 0 · servers that see what you watch: 0

How it works.

iPhone. A Safari blocker cleans the websites; the real apps get a daily allowance, notifications and all, until it runs out.

Android. A clean browser, plus a feed guard inside the real apps that skips ads and stops the next Short or Reel.

Chrome & Mac. The same rules on your laptop.
