observation

Idyll can’t read your pages.

On iPhone, Safari applies Idyll’s rules itself, and iOS shows it can’t read or send anything from the pages you visit. You log in to the real sites, never inside Idyll. No account, nothing leaves your phone.

checked: 0 trackers.

method

How it works

iPhone: a Safari blocker for the websites, and a daily allowance for the real apps; messages and notifications work until it runs out.

Android: a clean browser, and a feed guard inside the real apps that skips ads and stops the next Short or Reel.

Chrome & Mac: the same rules on your laptop.

limits

What Idyll can’t do

Nothing can change what’s inside the official apps on iPhone; Apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. So Idyll cleans up the websites, and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
