fig. 4Dates to be announced

Coming soon.

To iPhone, Android, Chrome and Mac. No download yet, and no waiting list to join: this page will change when it's ready.

  1. iPhone

    Coming soon

    The websites, cleaned. A Safari content blocker takes the feeds out of YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X in the browser. Safari applies the rules itself, and iOS shows that Idyll can't read or send anything from the pages. The optional Idyll Plus extension also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos.

    The apps, on an allowance. The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time. They work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

  2. Android

    Coming soon

    A clean built-in browser for each site, plus a feed guard inside the real apps that presses Skip ad and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

  3. Chrome and Mac

    Coming soon

    The same rules on a laptop, so the feed doesn't just move to a bigger screen.

The honest bit

What it can't do on iPhone.

No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone: Apple keeps apps sealed off. Anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy that can get accounts flagged. So Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
