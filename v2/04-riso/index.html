iPhone

A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules itself, and iOS shows that Idyll can't read or send anything from the pages. The optional Idyll Plus extension also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos.

The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time. They work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

Free.For good.

No subscription. No trial that turns into one. No ads. No data sold.

Idyll will never charge. We believe a tool for spending less time being sold to shouldn't sell you anything.

Your logins stay yours

You log in to the real sites and apps, never inside Idyll. There's no account to make. Nothing leaves your phone, and there are zero trackers.

What it can't do

No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone: Apple keeps apps sealed off. Anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy that can get accounts flagged. Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
