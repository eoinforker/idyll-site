table bHow it works, per device

No download yet. Every platform is in progress.

devicemethodstatus
iPhone

A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules, and iOS shows that Idyll can't read or send anything from the pages.

Optional: Idyll Plus, an extension that also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos.

The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time. They work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

 coming soon
Android

A clean built-in browser for each site, plus a feed guard inside the real apps that presses Skip ad and stops a swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

Free, for good. No subscription, no trial that turns into one, no ads, no data sold. Idyll will never charge. Something that gives you your time back shouldn't come with a bill.

noteTolerances

No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. Anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy, and that can get accounts flagged.

So on iPhone, Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
