Which cygnet would win on the App Store?

Twenty-three halftone icons, graded on what can be measured before launch, against the real icons of the ten apps Idyll will sit next to in search (SocialLite, Opal, one sec, BePresent, ScreenZen, Brick, Unscroll, Clearspace, Forest, Freedom).

The grades

Score out of 100 weighs: small-size legibility (40%: how different the bird and its background look at 29 pixels, perceptual colour difference, higher is clearer), distinctiveness (25%: colour distance from the nearest competitor's icon), home-screen pop (20%: worst-case contrast against a dark and a light wallpaper) and colour (15%: saturation, which draws the eye in a grid).

How to know for sure

These grades predict, they don't prove. The App Store's Product Page Optimisation lets you test up to three alternative icons against your original with real visitors, and Google Play's store listing experiments do the same. Launch with White on magenta, and test the two close-ups (orange on blue, pink on blue) against it in the first weeks. If the pink question worries you, that test answers it with real installs.

The halftone texture shows on the App Store page and in Spotlight at large sizes; on the home screen it reads as a clean flat silhouette, which is what you want.
