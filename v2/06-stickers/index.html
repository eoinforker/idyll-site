Directions for use

every platform is coming soon. nothing to download yet.

iPhone coming soon

A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules itself, and iOS shows that Idyll can’t read or send anything from the pages you visit.

Idyll Plus, if you want it, also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos. The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time: they work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

Android coming soon

A clean built-in browser for each site. Inside the real apps, a feed guard presses “Skip ad” for you and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

Chrome and Mac coming soon

The same rules, on a laptop.

Adhesive

Sticks to nothing of yours. You log in to the real sites and apps, never inside Idyll. No account. Nothing leaves your phone. Zero trackers.

Caution

No app can change what’s inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone; Apple keeps apps sealed off. Anyone who says otherwise ships a modified copy that can get accounts flagged. Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.

Price

Free, for good.

No subscription, no trial that turns into one, no ads, no data sold. Idyll will never charge. We’d rather say it plainly than print it small.
