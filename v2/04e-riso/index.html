No subscription, no ads, no data sold. Idyll will never charge.

The small print. On iPhone, a Safari content blocker cleans the websites (Safari applies the rules, and iOS shows Idyll can't read or send anything from the pages), the optional Idyll Plus extension skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos, and the real apps get a daily Screen Time allowance that pauses them until midnight. On Android, a clean browser for each site and a feed guard inside the real apps; on Chrome and Mac, the same rules on a laptop. You log in to the real sites, never inside Idyll: no account, nothing leaves your phone, zero trackers. Apple keeps the official YouTube and Instagram apps sealed, so no app can change what's inside them; anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy that can get accounts flagged, which is why Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
