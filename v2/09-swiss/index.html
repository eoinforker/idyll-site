Where it runs

One video, not the feed after it.

iPhone

Coming soon

A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules, and iOS shows that the blocker cannot read or send anything from the page.

Idyll Plus, an optional extension, also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos.

The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time. They work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

Android

Coming soon

A clean built-in browser for each site.

Inside the real apps, a feed guard presses “Skip ad” and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

Chrome and Mac

Coming soon

The same rules, on a laptop.

The rest of the evening, which was the point.

Privacy

You log in to the real sites and apps, never inside Idyll. No account. Nothing leaves your phone. Zero trackers.

Free,for good.

No subscription. No trial that turns into one. No ads. No data sold.

Idyll will never charge. A tool for being sold to less should not be for sale itself. That was settled first, before anything else was built.

The honest limit

No app can change what is inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. Anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy, which can get accounts flagged.

So Idyll cleans the websites, and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
