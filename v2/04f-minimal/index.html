How it works

iPhone

In Safari, a content blocker cleans YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X. Safari applies the rules itself, so Idyll can't read anything on the page.

The official apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time. They work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

The honest limit: no app can change what's inside the official apps on iPhone, because Apple keeps them sealed off.

Android

A clean built-in browser for each site, plus a feed guard inside the real apps that presses Skip ad and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel.

The one a friend sent still plays.

Chrome and Mac

The same rules on a laptop.

Everywhere, you log in to the real sites and apps, never inside Idyll. No account. No trackers. Nothing leaves your device.
