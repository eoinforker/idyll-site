    Where itworks.

    On your phone and your laptop, in the apps you already use. Each one is coming soon.

    iPhoneSafari and Screen Time

    A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules, and iOS shows that Idyll can't read or send anything from the pages. The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time: they work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight. The optional Idyll Plus extension also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos.

    Coming soon

    AndroidBrowser and feed guard

    A clean built-in browser for each site, plus a feed guard inside the real apps that presses Skip ad and stops a swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

    No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off. Anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy that can get accounts flagged.

    So Idyll cleans the websites, and gives the apps a daily allowance instead.

    You log in tothe real thing.

    Never inside Idyll. You sign in to the real sites and apps, exactly as you do now. Idyll only decides what gets shown.

    • Accounts to make None
    • Passwords Idyll sees None
    • Trackers Zero
    • Data that leaves your phone None

    On iPhone, the rules run inside Safari itself. Safari applies them, and iOS shows plainly that Idyll can't read or send anything from the pages you visit.

    There is no Idyll account, no sign-up form and no server keeping notes on you. Nothing to sell, because nothing is collected.

    Free.For good.

    No subscription, no trial that turns into one, no ads, no data sold. Idyll will never charge. It's a small tool that does one decent thing, and it should belong to everyone.

    Every platform is coming soon. There's no download link yet and no form to fill in, so there's nothing to give us. Come back and it'll be here.
