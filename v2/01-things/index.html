The real apps

The apps get an allowance, not a ban.

On iPhone, Idyll gives the real apps a daily allowance through Screen Time. They work as usual, notifications and all, until the minutes run out. Then they pause until midnight.

On Android, a feed guard works inside the real apps: it presses "Skip ad" for you and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

Privacy, and the honest part

You never log in to Idyll.

You log in to the real sites and apps, the way you always have. No Idyll account, nothing leaves your phone, zero trackers.

It can't read your pages

On iPhone, Safari applies Idyll's rules itself, and iOS shows that the blocker can't read or send anything from the pages you visit.

What no app can do on iPhone

No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off. Anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy, and that can get your account flagged.

So Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.

No subscription. No trial that turns into one. No ads, and no data sold. Idyll will never charge, and we're proud to say so.
