On puttingthe phonedown

Idyll takes the ads, the Shorts, the Reels and the algorithmic feeds out of YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X, and leaves the parts you came for. It is free, and it will stay that way. Here is what it does, how it does it, and where it has to stop.

Words by the people making Idyll · About five minutes · iPhone, Android, Chrome and Mac, all coming soon

Works with

There is a kind of evening that ends with a phone held above your face in the dark, forty minutes into videos you never chose. Very few of us opened the app for that. We opened it to answer a message, to watch the clip a friend had sent, to check whether the group had moved Thursday to Friday. The feed was simply waiting on the other side of the thing we wanted.

Idyll is a small, free tool built around that gap. It takes the ads, YouTube Shorts, Reels and algorithmic feeds out of YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X, and keeps what people came for: messages, search, the one video a friend sends you (that video, and not the feed that would normally follow it), your YouTube subscriptions, the people you follow on X, your Facebook friends in the order they posted, and your groups. Social, without the scroll.

On an iPhoneIdyll works as a Safari content blocker. It hands Safari a list of rules, and Safari does the cleaning. iOS itself tells you that a content blocker cannot read or send anything from the pages you visit.1 An optional extension, Idyll Plus, goes a little further: it skips video ads and turns Shorts back into ordinary videos, with a play bar and an end.

The apps are a different matter.2 For those, Idyll uses Screen Time to give each one a daily allowance. They work normally, notifications and all, until your minutes run out, and then they pause until midnight. It is a gentler limit than deleting them, and a firmer one than willpower at eleven at night.

It is easier to show than to describe. Turn the page.

On putting the phone downIdyll
“

Idyll is free, for good. No subscription, no trial that turns into one, no ads, and no data sold.

On AndroidYou get a clean built-in browser for each site, and a feed guard inside the real apps. The guard presses “Skip ad” for you and stops the swipe into the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays; it is the one after it that does not arrive.

On a laptopThe same rules come to Chrome and to the Mac, so the desk is as quiet as the pocket.

On privacyYou log in to the real sites and the real apps, never inside Idyll. There is no Idyll account to make, because there is nothing for one to hold. Nothing leaves your phone. There are zero trackers.

On moneyIdyll will never charge. We say that plainly, because it is a belief we hold, and it was decided before anything else was. A tool for spending less of your life being sold to should not be something that is sold to you, by subscription or by the slow trade of your attention and your data. So there is no price, now or later.

On whenEvery version, for iPhone, Android, Chrome and Mac, is coming soon. There is no download link yet and no mailing list, so there is nothing to sign up for. When it is ready, this page will say so.■
