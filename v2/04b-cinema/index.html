iPhoneComing soon

A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules itself, and iOS shows that Idyll can’t read or send anything from the pages you visit. The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time: they work, notifications and all, until your minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

Idyll Plus, an optional extension, also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos.

AndroidComing soon

A clean built-in browser for each site, plus a feed guard inside the real apps. It presses Skip ad for you and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

Chrome and MacComing soon

The same rules on your laptop, so the feed doesn’t simply move to a bigger screen.

Sealed

The honest bit.

No app can change what’s inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off from each other, and anyone who says otherwise is shipping a modified copy that can get your account flagged. So Idyll cleans the websites, and gives the apps a daily limit instead.

You log in tothe real sites.

Never inside Idyll. Your passwords go where they always went, and Idyll sits quietly in between, taking things away.

  1. No account.There’s nothing to sign up for, so there’s no Idyll profile of you anywhere.
  2. Nothing leaves your phone.The rules run on the device. What you look at stays between you and the site you’re on.
  3. Zero trackers.Not one. Not even the polite kind that counts visits.
