Where it works

On your phone, and on your laptop

iPhone Coming soon

A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules itself, and iOS shows that the blocker can't read or send anything from the pages you visit.

Idyll Plus, an optional extension, also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos.

The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time. They work as usual, notifications and all, until the minutes run out. Then they pause until midnight.

Android Coming soon

A clean built-in browser for each site.

Inside the real apps, a feed guard presses "Skip ad" for you and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel. The one a friend sent still plays.

Chrome and Mac Coming soon

The same rules on a laptop, so the quiet follows you to the bigger screen.

The honest part

What no app can do on iPhone

No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. Anyone who says otherwise is shipping a modified copy of those apps, and that can get your account flagged.

So Idyll does the two things that are possible and safe: it cleans the websites, and it gives the apps a daily limit.

Privacy

You never log in to Idyll

You log in to the real sites and apps, the way you always have. Idyll has no account to make, nothing leaves your phone, and there are no trackers. None.

What it costs

Free, for good.

No subscription. No trial that turns into one. No ads, and no data sold. Idyll will never charge, because a quieter phone shouldn't be something you pay rent on.
