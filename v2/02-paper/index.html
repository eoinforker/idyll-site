on every screen.quietly.

the same rules wherever you are. all of it runs on your device: no account, nothing leaves your phone, zero trackers.

iphone coming soon

clean websites, and a daily allowance for the apps.

a safari content blocker cleans the sites. safari applies the rules itself, and ios shows that idyll can't read or send anything from the pages you visit. the optional idyll plus extension also skips video ads and turns shorts into normal videos.

the real apps get a daily allowance through screen time. they work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

android coming soon

a clean browser for each site, and a guard inside the apps.

the feed guard presses "skip ad" for you and stops the swipe to the next short or reel. the one a friend sent still plays.

chrome and mac coming soon

the same rules on a laptop.

the same sites, cleaned the same way, wherever you sit down.

privacy by design

you log in to the real sites, never inside idyll.

there is no idyll account. nothing leaves your phone. there are no trackers, not one.

free, for good.

no subscription, no trial that turns into one, no ads, no data sold. idyll will never charge. a calmer phone should not be something you pay for.

an honest limit

no app can change what's inside the official youtube or instagram apps on iphone. apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. anyone who says otherwise is shipping a modified copy of the app, and that can get your account flagged.

so idyll cleans the websites, and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
