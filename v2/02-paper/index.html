on every screen,without asking for your password.

you log in to the real sites and apps, never inside idyll. there is no account to make. nothing leaves your phone, and there are no trackers. not one.

iphone coming soon

clean websites, and a daily allowance for the apps.

instagram
18 of 30 minutes left today
then it pauses until midnight

a safari content blocker cleans the sites. safari applies the rules itself, and ios shows that idyll can't read or send anything from your pages. the optional idyll plus extension also skips video ads and turns shorts into normal videos.

the real apps get a daily allowance through screen time. they work, notifications and all, until the minutes run out.

android coming soon

a clean browser for each site, and a guard inside the apps.

the feed guard presses "skip ad" for you and stops the swipe to the next short or reel. the one a friend sent still plays.

chrome and mac coming soon

the same rules on a laptop.

the same sites, cleaned the same way, wherever you sit down.

free, for good.

a calmer phone shouldn't be a subscription. idyll will never charge, and it will never sell what it doesn't collect.

no subscriptionno trial that turns into oneno adsno data sold

what idyll can't do.said plainly.

no app can change what's inside the official youtube or instagram apps on iphone. apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. anyone who says otherwise is shipping a modified copy of the app, and that can get your account flagged.

so on iphone, idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
