Idyll

Social without the scroll.

Idyll takes the ads, Shorts, Reels and algorithmic feeds out of YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X. What you went there for stays.

What goes

YouTube
Video ads, Shorts, the recommended home feed, Up next.
Instagram
Sponsored posts, the Reels feed, the Explore grid.
Facebook
Reels, Watch, the algorithmic feed.
X
For you, promoted posts, trends, who to follow.

What stays

Your messages. Search. Whatever a friend sends you: one video, not the feed that comes after it.

Your YouTube subscriptions. Following on X. Facebook friends in order, and your groups.

Where

iPhone
A Safari content blocker cleans the sites; Safari applies the rules, and iOS shows Idyll can't read or send anything from your pages. Optional Idyll Plus skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos. The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time, then pause until midnight.
Android
A clean browser for each site, and a feed guard in the real apps that presses Skip ad and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel.
Laptop
The same rules in Chrome and on Mac.

No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off, and anyone claiming otherwise ships a modified copy that can get accounts flagged. So Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a limit.

Privacy

You log in to the real sites and apps, never inside Idyll. There is no account. Nothing leaves your phone. Zero trackers.

Free, for good

No subscription. No trial that turns into one. No ads, and no data sold.

Idyll will never charge.
