How it works on each phone

On iPhone

A Safari content blocker cleans the websites. Safari applies the rules itself, and iOS shows that Idyll can't read or send anything from the pages you visit.

The optional Idyll Plus extension also skips video ads and turns Shorts into normal videos. The real apps get a daily allowance through Screen Time: they work, notifications and all, until your minutes run out, then pause until midnight.

On Android

A clean built-in browser for each site, plus a feed guard inside the real apps. It presses Skip ad for you and stops the swipe to the next Short or Reel.

The one a friend sent still plays.

An honest limit

No app can change what's inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone. Apple keeps apps sealed off, and anyone claiming otherwise is shipping a modified copy that can get accounts flagged. So Idyll cleans the websites and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
