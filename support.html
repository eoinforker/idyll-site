Common questions

Is it really free? Yes. No subscription, no trial, no ads, nothing to unlock. Idyll will never charge.

Why can't Idyll remove Reels from the Instagram app on iPhone? Apple doesn't let any app change what's inside another app. So on iPhone, Idyll gives you a clean version of each site inside Idyll, cleans them in Safari, and can put a daily limit on the real apps.

I can't sign in to a site inside Idyll. You're signing in directly with that service, so a problem is almost always with the account itself. Try resetting your password or another two-factor method, then try again.

Android stopped blocking. Check the Accessibility permission for Idyll is still on, as some phones turn it off after updates. On Samsung, Xiaomi and OnePlus, also allow auto-start and turn off battery optimisation for Idyll.

How do I sign out of everything? In Idyll settings, choose Sign out everywhere.
