idyll free, for good

Social, without the scroll.

Idyll takes the ads, Shorts, Reels and algorithmic feeds out of YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X. Your messages, your search and whatever friends send you stay.

iPhone · Android · Chrome & Mac · coming soon

What changes

youtube
Video ads, Shorts, the recommended feed. Your subscriptions and search stay.
instagram
Sponsored posts, the Reels feed, the Explore grid. Messages, search and the reel a friend sent you stay.
facebook
Reels, Watch, the algorithmic feed. Friends, in order, groups and Marketplace stay.
x
For you, promoted posts, trends. Following stays.

Idyll can’t read your pages.

On iPhone, Idyll hands Safari a list of rules and Safari does the rest; iOS shows it can’t read or send anything from the pages you visit. You log in to the real sites and the real apps, never inside Idyll. There’s no account and nothing leaves your phone.

How it works

iphone
A Safari blocker cleans up the websites. The real apps get a daily allowance: messages and notifications keep working until it runs out, then Screen Time pauses them until midnight.
android
A clean browser for each site, and a feed guard inside the real apps that skips video ads and stops a swipe to the next Short or Reel.
chrome & mac
The same rules on your laptop.

Free, for good.

no subscription · no ads · no data sold · ever

What Idyll can’t do

No app can change what’s inside the official YouTube or Instagram apps on iPhone; Apple keeps apps sealed off from each other. Anyone who says otherwise is shipping a modified copy of those apps, which can get your account flagged. So Idyll cleans up the websites, and gives the apps a daily limit instead.
